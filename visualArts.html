U7A2: Circles and Lines
Studio Project
——————————
I tried to make this look like bubbles were overlapping, red and blue ones, with a split down the middle. I wanted it to also look like some kind of comparison between light and dark mode of devices, hence the extreme color split, yet I still kept the color palette analogous (purple being the central color).
Now, with all this said, would I have made this digitally if I had that skill set so this could be some abstract background? Yes, very much so, I would love to see this with light shining through from the back to make a nice background, kinda similar to those seen in Windows Vista or 7.
